| **Quick run** | `python3 -m pytest modules/fleet-control-plane/tests/test_internal_dns_authority.py -q -x` |

## Sampling Rate

- After each repo-code task: focused pytest.
- After each prepare/checkpoint/apply group: preflight, layer verifier, then rollback/reapply evidence.
- No live apply starts unless its immediately preceding checkpoint approval hash is current.
- Before release: direct, resilience, CoreDNS, AdGuard, every client, primary-loss/recovery, rollback, latency, secret scan, and CPU-capped suite are PASS.
