The three tasks form one evidence chain: server-admitted one-service sizing contracts → sequential live measurement/removal and memory freeze → creation/application of final 2+2 resources. Splitting that chain would allow final values to be authored without the exact sizing hash/headroom receipt. No value may be selected by planner discretion.
